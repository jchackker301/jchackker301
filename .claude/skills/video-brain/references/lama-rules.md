# Lama's approval rules

Taken from her real feedback in the Marketing Operations chat (Jul-Oct 2026). Dates show where each rule came from.

## CHECKLIST (Mode 4 goes through these in order)
1. **Hook in 2 s**: something happens or is asked in the first 2 s. No slow intro. ("The beginning is tooooo slow and long" 28 Sep; "too slow paced" 24 Sep)
2. **Pace**: no shot longer than about 3 s, no dragging middle section; the "solution" part is short and clear. ("make your videos more interactive and faster" 30 Sep)
3. **Facts**: every claim matches ducon-facts.md: supervise, not install; design and manufacture; Paver Care Packages; no pebbles; correct tools. (5 Oct, 21 Aug, 31 Jul)
4. **Right brand and logo**: an ODL video uses the ODL logo; Industries/Blocks content shows buildings and blocks, not only ODL. (24 Aug, 12 Aug)
5. **Right place**: the showroom/project shown is the real one; scenes fit the property (townhouse = small yard). (10 Aug, 29 Sep)
6. **CTA at the end**: voiceover or text CTA, a soft invite (visit the showroom / chat with Saif). Never end without one. (5 Oct, 7 Aug)
7. **Contact**: Saif content shows the Saif WhatsApp number; no QR code on reels. (3 Oct, 13 Oct)
8. **Voiceover**: a matching, natural voice; the same voice across one series. Use only voices we own or have the rights to. (7 Aug, 26 Sep)
9. **Subtitles**: Arabic subtitles correct (have a native speaker check them), one line at a time, synced to the voiceover. (7 Sep, 24 Sep)
10. **Copy sounds human**: no AI-sounding taglines, no lines that "make no sense", lines do not all start the same way, no wordy text. (1, 2 and 5 Oct; 23 Sep)
11. **Dialogue sounds natural**: no office words in family scenes ("Okay team", "Noted"). (6 Oct)
12. **Music**: fits the mood; change it if it is flat. (23 Jul)
13. **Thumbnail**: every YouTube/long video has a good thumbnail. (14 Jul, 18 Aug)
14. **Versions**: reel 9:16 + landscape for screens when it is a project video; a low-res copy for WhatsApp/status. (19 Aug, 2 Oct)
15. **Clean frame**: plate numbers blurred, no wrong labels (sitting vs dining), text not covered. (5 Oct)
16. **AI glitches**: AI shots must match reality (no "before installation" look in an after shot, no wrong showroom). (13 Jul, 10 Aug)
17. **Delivery**: server link in the message, file saved in the right folder, Asana task updated with link and deadline. (27 Aug, 6 Aug, 14 Oct)

## What Lama praised (do more of this)
- AI-recreated project videos that bring spaces to life (people using the areas, day-to-night). (27 Jul)
- An AI avatar speaking in videos. (28 Jul)
- The SADU themes direction. (22 Sep)
- Sharing useful ideas and tools with the team. (7 Aug)
- Showing how a space develops (old seating -> SADU seating, the design -> reality transition). (29 Sep)
- Simple, clear voiceover endings with the Duconize line. (7 Aug, 5 Oct)

## Team process rules
- One calendar: work from the approved monthly content calendar; update it (done / pending / assignee). (21 Sep, 31 Aug)
- Monthly strategy meeting -> calendar -> production; weekly meeting with reports ready. (1 Oct)
- Asana always updated: tasks, completed links, deadlines. (14 Oct)
- Update without being chased: report status before Lama asks. (12 Aug)
