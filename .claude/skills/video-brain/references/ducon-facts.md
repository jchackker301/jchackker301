## What Ducon DOES and does NOT do (most common mistakes)
| Say this | Never say this |
|---|---|
| We **design and manufacture** pavers, pergolas and products | "we sell pergolas" or "pergolas are ours" as if bought in |
| We **supervise** installation | "we install" or "we handle installation" |
| **Paver Care Packages** | "seasonal care" |
| ANSAHAK = Ducon's installation hub (consultation and supervision) | Ansahak engineers design or choose paver materials and textures. That is the job of Designers and Sales Reps |
| Real installer tools only | tools our installers do not use |
| — | pebble walkways (we don't do that) |
