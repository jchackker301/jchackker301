# Idea bank

## Hook formulas (each idea in Mode 1 uses a different one)
1. **Before -> After snap**: bare yard, hard cut to the finished space on the beat. Text: "Same yard. Now Duconized."
2. **Question to the viewer**: "Would you cook here every weekend?" over the hero shot.
3. **Mistake / myth**: "3 outdoor kitchen mistakes to avoid" (general tips, no Ducon claims). An education reel.
4. **POV / FPV fly-through**: a camera flies from the gate to the pool in one move (Full AI or drone).
5. **Unexpected start**: plane window -> showroom, phone screen -> real backyard, sketch -> 3D -> reality.
6. **Build-up layers**: show what goes underneath (foundation layers rising up to the finished paver). Fits the "See what goes underneath it all" line.
7. **Day -> Night**: same space at sunset, lights on (Dulight).
8. **Problem -> Ducon fix** (Bu Khalid style): one real problem (heat, privacy, drainage, stains), then the solution.
9. **Number hook**: "4 million tons a year" / "1 point of contact". Only real numbers from ducon-facts.
10. **SAIF demo**: phone shows a backyard photo sent to Saif -> design appears -> it becomes real. End with the Saif number.
11. **Human moment**: family/friends using the space (fits the yard size), no office dialogue.
12. **Detail ASMR**: close textures, water on pavers, the shampoo cleaning stains, no voice, strong sound design.

## Formats by tier
- **A (Full AI)**: FPV tours, build-up layers, day->night, SAIF demo, AI avatar presenter, impossible camera moves.
- **B (Mix)**: real project footage + AI fill shots (people added, night version, missing angles).
- **C (Normal)**: real shoot edits: project reveal, showroom walk-through, factory process, client handover.

## Series that can run every week
- **Duconize in 30 s**: one real project per week, before -> after + CTA.
- **What goes underneath**: the 4 foundation levels, one per episode.
- **8 Areas**: walkway, driveway, parking, sitting, dining, kitchen, pool, play: one area per reel.
- **SADU Stories**: heritage pattern -> modern majlis.
- **Saif Did This**: a real user photo -> Saif design -> the real build.
- **Care Tips**: Paver Care Packages + Hardscape Shampoo, how to keep it new.
- **Factory to Backyard** (Industries): Recover -> Recycle -> Manufacture -> Apply -> Deliver.
